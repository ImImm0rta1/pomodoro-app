# Pomodoro App/ Break Reminder
App that reminds every X minutes and starts timer for 30(X)seconds to look away from screen

Name and logo: the app is now “Break Reminder”. The logo fuses your pink clock reference with a tomato. It’s a clock whose ring has a wedge missing (the break), with tomato leaves on top, drawn in code like before.

Layout: the top bar is gone. A floating bottom dock has Home, Schedules and Settings.

Home: a progress ring counts down to the next alert, with a 1-hour pause, a master on/off switch, the next three alert times, and a count of breaks left today. The ring redraws once a second only while the app is open.

Schedules: each schedule has its own Sound switch and Vibrate switch. First alert vibrates three times and the second twice. Turn Sound off for vibration only, or Vibrate off for sound only. Sound files are chosen per schedule as before.

Themes (Settings): five, based on your references. Ember: black with a red-orange glow. Matrix: graphite, dot-matrix digits and a blue-yellow border. Blush: light pink. Lavender: periwinkle and coral. Mono: grey with black and amber. They’re only colour settings in code, so they add no weight.

Power: unchanged. One alarm at a time, with nothing running between alerts.
